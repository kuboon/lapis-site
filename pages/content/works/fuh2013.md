From Up Here is a simple and beautiful musical by Aaron Lee Lambert, originally developed and produced by Perfect Pitch in June 2012.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L9Wv59LlQ1o

It explores time, change and the choices that shape the lives of five very different people whose paths cross in the most unlikely of ways, asking us the question: what makes life worth living?
This new production presented by Théatre Lapis will be its London premiere.
