Our Artistic Director worked as the assistant director to Marcello Magni, a founding member of Complicite, in "Tinker Bell in Shoji-Land" written by Hideki Noda at Tokyo Metropolitan Theatre. The show was filled with physical works and puppetry. He also worked as the vocal supervisor.
