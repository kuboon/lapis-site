In June 2016, Our Artistic Director Yojiro Ichikawa directed the Japanese premiere production of Lloyd Webber's " Tell Me on a Sunday" at The New National Theatre Tokyo, in collaboration with Megumi Hamada, who originated Nala in The Lion King, the title role of AIDA, and Elphaba in Wicked in Japan.

The production won OUTSTANDING ACTRESS IN LEADING ROLE AWARD in Yomiuri Drama Awards.
