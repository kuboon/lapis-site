As part of the concert 'Echoes of Hope' marking the 15th anniversary of The Great East Japan Earthquake Disaster, Yojiro directed a staged reading 'The String Telephone' written by Toru Yamamoto, based on a novella by Juri Sato.

朗読劇「糸電話」-The String Telephone-

- **原作小説：佐藤珠莉　The original novel: Juri Sato**
- **脚本：山本透　Playwright:Toru Yamamoto**
- **演出：市川洋二郎　Director: Yojiro Ichikawa**
- **俳優：黒田はるか・安達原 旭・リオ　アシザワ　Actor: Haruka Kuroda, Asahi Adachihara, Leo Ashizawa**
- **ピアノ：松元 沙綾　piano: Saaya Matsumoto**
