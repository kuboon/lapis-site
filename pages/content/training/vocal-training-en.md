## ＜Lesson Details＞

Want to improve your singing, prepare for auditions, or develop greater confidence as a performer?

Yojiro Ichikawa is a professional vocal coach with experience across the UK, the US, and Japan. Drawing on his work as a vocal consultant or a vocal supervisor in major productions including *My Neighbour Totoro*, *Allegiance*, *Titanic*, *Tell Me on a Sunday*, and *Tinker Bell in Shoji-Land*, he helps singers and actors develop vocal technique, storytelling, and performance skills.

Trained in a wide range of vocal styles—from musical theatre, pop, and rock to classical singing—Yojiro tailors each session to the individual needs and goals of his students, whether they are preparing for auditions, working on a role, or simply singing for enjoyment.

His teaching combines vocal technique with principles from the Alexander Technique, helping students recognise and release unnecessary tension, improve physical awareness, and discover a freer, more natural voice. By developing greater awareness of how the body works, students can sing with more ease, freedom, and confidence.

Over the years, Yojiro has helped students prepare for successful auditions for drama schools, youth theatre programmes, and theatre productions. His coaching focuses not only on vocal technique, but also on storytelling, confidence, and authentic performance.

Yojiro also offers coaching in Japanese pronunciation for those interested in singing Anime, J-Pop, or Japanese musical theatre repertoire, helping students perform songs with greater accuracy and understanding.

Students of all ages and experience levels are welcome, from complete beginners to working professionals.

#### ○Session Menu

A) ‘FUN’ Course （£50/h, Students:£40/h）

This course focuses on the enjoyment of singing. From basic vocal techniques to storytelling through a song, Yojiro will teach you the most suitable way of singing for your ability. Suitable for everyone.

**B) ‘TECHNIQUE’ Course**

B-1) Voice Enhancement Course（£50/h, Students:£40/h）
Learn how to be aware of your physicality, we will work on removing all the unneeded tensions and habits from your body, so that you can generate a deep and relaxing, yet strong voice. This session is effective for your public speaking as well. Yojiro will deal with your voice precisely note by note, from your belt to falsetto voice. Suitable for those looking to progress their vocal techniques.

B-2) Advanced Course（£65/90mins, Students:£55/90mins）
In addition to what we offer in “Voice Enhancement”, we will also work on how you can apply the techniques to singing a song. You will also learn the range of expression you can do with the song. This is perfect to develop your repertoire for auditions.
